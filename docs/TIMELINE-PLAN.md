# Ostermill site · the timeline · plan v1

2026-08-20. The "big slider that walks the reader through the project timeline and
milestones" (Yoram, 08-19). Every date below is pulled from CASE-FILE-BIBLE.md; nothing
is invented. Where the bible is silent, the gap is named instead of filled.

## Placement, and the no-spoiler rule

The timeline is a CASE-FILE surface, not an intro surface. It lives behind the case-file
door in the reader chrome, because a timeline is inherently the whole arc. The public
Ostermill site keeps only company history (1935, 1968, 2014); persona profiles introduce
people as of day one and link forward to nothing. One authorized tease exists on the
corporate site ("This year: the receivables project"), and it is the door, not a leak.

## The shape: seventeen months, five bands, one road

(Framing corrected 08-20, re-based 08-23: the PROJECT is sixteen weeks, demonstration
to rung-2 launch; the rest is the product's operating life. The road carries the bracket
"idea to production, 16 weeks · governance, ongoing." See MEETING-CALENDAR-CANON.)

A horizontal slider, left to right, drawn in the site's line-art hand. Structure:

- **A pre-story shoulder** (the previous November: the reclassification script begins
  running unnoticed; February: Tom proposes Agent v1). Drawn in lighter ink; the reader
  learns later that the story started before anyone was watching, which is the book's
  point about substrate.
- **Five phase bands**: Decide · Design · Prove · Observe · Operate, as labeled segments
  of the road. Phase transitions are gates, drawn as such (the go memo, the launch, the
  rung moves), because the book's argument is that the phases hand off through artifacts.
- **Year 1 and Year 2** at different zoom: year 1 is week-dense (March alone has five
  nodes), year 2 is month-dense. The slider compresses honestly: a wide March, a thin
  November, which itself teaches where the decisions were.

Each node: mono date chip, title, one canon sentence, and links into the evidence
(exhibit page, transcript, prototype, eval run). Nodes with a red edge are the incident
thread (the unset dispute flag: three strikes across two years). The current node under
the cursor gets the one yellow marker; everything else stays ink.

## Interaction, kept honest

Slider drag or arrow keys; deep links per node (#mar-26) so the book can point at
moments; every node links to evidence rather than describing it twice. No autoplay, no
animation on the incident thread. On mobile the road rotates vertical. Data is one
timeline.json in the same canon-file family as the golden set, and doubles as the
public download, consistent with "the data layer is the product twice."
