PERSONA RECORD · FORM PD-2CARD 1 OF 3

Ruth Vaughn

THE SUPERVISING USER
SENIOR RECEIVABLES CLERK
COLUMBUS · 22 YEARS

"I can tell you why I held every one of them. Nobody ever asked before March."

220drafts / week, ordinary queue
90sbudget per draft
41holds / week, hers alone
3actions: approve · edit · reject

Goals

  • See why the agent judged a case ordinary, not just what it drafted
  • Reject as cheaply as she approves
  • Know what happens to an account after a no
  • Keep working accounts one day a week; the eye is the job

Frustrations

  • Drafts read fluent and confident whether right or wrong
  • The queue rewards speed; thoroughness is invisible
  • Freight-line accounts are wrong in a way she can predict
  • A screen that shows the answer trains her to agree with it

What she knows that no system holds

  • Which late payers are certain and which are becoming a problem
  • Names from the trade press, hallways, and nineteen years of calls
  • When the written policy is wrong for this account, this month

Designs this card produced

  • The strongest-objection slot on the approval screen
  • "Routes to R. Vaughn, typically same day" under the reject control
  • Reject reasons as a fixed list, feeding the eval set

Her old job was deciding. Her new job is catching us deciding badly, and the screen either serves that or performs it.
